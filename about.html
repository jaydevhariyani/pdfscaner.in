Mission & Vision

pdfscaner.in is an independent web utility built to deliver desktop-grade document processing and financial market feeds directly into the modern web browser. Founded and operated by Jaydevbhai Hariyani in Surat, Gujarat, India, the platform eliminates the need to download bloated smartphone apps filled with intrusive tracking and paywalls.

Why Choose Our Tools?

🔒 Zero-Knowledge Local Architecture All camera captures, document crops, and PDF compilation routines run entirely inside your browser memory using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Your documents are never uploaded to any cloud server.
⚡ Instant Cross-Platform Utility Fully optimized for Android Chrome, iOS Safari, and desktop browsers without requiring app store downloads or logins.
📈 Real-Time Market Integrations Direct spot rate intelligence for gold, silver, and world currencies updated live for Indian traders, importers, and international merchants.
