About pdfscaner.in

Welcome to pdfscaner.in, your fast, secure, and browser-based document scanning utility managed by Jaydevbhai Hariyani from Surat, Gujarat.

We built this tool to help users quickly capture multi-page physical documents, crop unnecessary edges, and convert them cleanly into standard A4 PDF files directly from their mobile or desktop browser without installing heavy apps.
