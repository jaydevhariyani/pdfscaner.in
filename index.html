About Us

Welcome to pdfscaner.in, your fast, secure, and browser-based document scanning utility managed by Jaydevbhai Hariyani from Surat, Gujarat.

We built this tool to help users quickly capture multi-page physical documents, crop unnecessary edges, and convert them clean into standard A4 PDF files directly from their mobile or desktop browser without installing heavy apps.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is 100% protected with pdfscaner.in:

  • Client-Side Processing: All image capture, cropping, and PDF compilation happen locally inside your browser memory.
  • No Server Uploads: Your scanned documents/images are never uploaded or stored on any remote server.
  • No Tracking: We do not collect or sell your personal document data.
