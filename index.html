Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How frequently are the metal prices updated?

Our feed connects to decentralized market rate feeds that update continuously throughout international trading hours. Click the "Refresh Live Prices" button anytime for instant spot sync.

Are local Indian taxes (GST / Making Charges) included?

The rates displayed show the pure international spot price calculated in INR. Jeweler making charges, import duties, and 3% GST applicable in India are not included in baseline bullion spot figures.

What is the difference between an Ounce and a Gram?

In precious metals, weight is measured in Troy Ounces. 1 Troy Ounce equals precisely 31.1035 grams (distinct from a standard food ounce of 28.3495 grams).
