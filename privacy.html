1. Data Minimization Guarantee

At pdfscaner.in, we fundamentally believe your sensitive personal and business documents belong solely to you. We have engineered our web application so that image acquisition, auto edge detection, and PDF generation execute exclusively inside your local device RAM.

2. Zero Server Transmission

  • No Cloud Storage: Images captured by your phone or webcam are never transmitted, compressed, or cached on external backend servers.
  • No Account Required: We do not ask for signups, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Once you close or reload the browser tab, all temporary canvas image buffers are completely wiped from device memory.

3. Cookies & Advertising

We may display network advertisements (e.g. Google AdSense) to keep the utility free. These partners may use anonymized cookies to serve relevant contextual ads according to standard web privacy practices.
